British Army Careers

Infantry Soldier

Front-line soldiers responsible for combat operations, peacekeeping and security missions worldwide.

Royal Engineer

Build bridges, roads and infrastructure while supporting military operations.

Intelligence Analyst

Collect and analyse information to support military decision-making.

Army Medic

Provide emergency healthcare and medical support to soldiers.

Royal Air Force Careers

RAF Pilot

Fly military aircraft during training, transport and operational missions.

RAF Police

Protect RAF personnel, bases and assets in the UK and overseas.

Aerospace Engineer

Maintain and repair sophisticated aircraft and aviation technology.

Intelligence Analyst

Analyse intelligence to support RAF operational planning.

Royal Navy Careers

Warfare Specialist

Operate advanced radar and weapons systems aboard Royal Navy vessels.

Engineering Technician

Maintain ships, submarines and naval equipment.

Royal Marine

Elite commandos capable of operating on land, sea and in extreme environments.

Medical Assistant

Provide healthcare support during naval operations worldwide.

How To Join The Armed Forces

  1. Research careers that match your interests and skills.
  2. Submit an online application.
  3. Complete aptitude or role-specific tests.
  4. Attend an interview and medical assessment.
  5. Pass fitness assessments.
  6. Receive an offer and start basic training.

Basic Entry Requirements

Age

Most roles require applicants to be at least 16 years old, with parental consent if under 18.

Fitness

Applicants must meet the physical standards required for their chosen role.

Medical Standards

Candidates must satisfy medical and health requirements.

Qualifications

Some specialist roles require GCSEs, A Levels or higher qualifications.
